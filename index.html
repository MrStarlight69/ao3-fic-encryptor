AO3 Fic Encryptor

Scramble the story in an AO3 HTML download with a cipher you design. Only the story text is encrypted (chapter titles and chapters, not the tags, summary or notes), and it appears right here on the page using only characters you can type on a standard keyboard. Anyone you give the cipher key to can turn it back. Everything happens in your browser: nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  1. On AO3, open a work and choose Download → HTML.
  2. Drop that file in Encrypt and press Encrypt.
  3. Send the text, and separately share the cipher key from the Cipher tab.
📖 Drop your AO3 .html file here or click to choose one (AO3: Download → HTML). Only the story itself gets encrypted. Got separate chapter files? Drop them all at once.

ID

Simplify characters turns everything that isn't on a keyboard into the closest keyboard spelling before encrypting: accents are dropped (é ñ ü ç → e n u c), letters like ß æ ø ł become ss ae o l, ligatures and fancy or fullwidth letters become normal ones, Greek and Cyrillic are spelled in Latin letters, curly quotes, dashes, …, « », × ™ © ° become " -- ... x (TM) (c) deg, and invisible control characters are removed. Emoji and other scripts (Chinese, Japanese, Arabic…) can't be spelled on a keyboard, so they are kept exactly either way. Turn it off to keep every character exactly as it is. Either way the encrypted text only uses keyboard characters and never shows escape codes: special characters are written as a few extra cipher characters, and decrypting turns them back.

    Nothing leaves your browser. Small settings are kept in a cookie and in local storage (so either one can restore them); big texts go in the browser's database (IndexedDB), which has room for long works; the open tab is kept for this browser session only.
